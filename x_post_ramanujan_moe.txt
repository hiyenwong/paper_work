Just dropped a new paper: RamanujanMoE-Topo 🚀

MoE layers scale poorly because expert communication is unstructured. What if experts sat on a graph with optimal connectivity?

Ramanujan graphs are expanders with the best possible spectral gap:
λ ≤ 2√(d-1) — provably optimal.

Key idea: Use Ramanujan graph adjacency as the expert communication backbone. Under fixed sparsity d, information propagates across all N experts in O(log_d N) rounds, matching the Ω(log_d N) information-theoretic lower bound for any d-sparse topology.

Two provable results:
1. Diffusion coverage: 1 - (2√(d-1)/d)^T after T steps
2. Mixing diameter: O(log_d N), proves near-optimality under sparsity constraint

This is purely a topology design — no claims about training loss convergence (gating, optimizer, load balancing are orthogonal).
